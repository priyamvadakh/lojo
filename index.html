FAQ

Curious about lojo?

What can lojo connect to?

Anywhere your knowledge already lives: Drive, Notion, Confluence, internal wikis, support ticketing, CRM, WhatsApp and Instagram conversations, call recordings, email, and release notes from GitHub. If a channel matters to you and isn't listed, ask us — connectors are the easy part.

How current is the knowledge base?

lojo watches its sources continuously. When pricing changes in a doc or a policy shifts in a ticket thread, the knowledge base updates without anyone exporting or copy-pasting a thing.

Does content go out without review?

No. Anything customer-facing goes through stakeholder validation first — humans sign off before content is published or distributed. You choose where the human checkpoints sit.

Who can see what?

Access follows your permissions. Teams see what they're meant to see, customers only ever see published, validated content, and your data is never used to train models for anyone else.

How long does setup take?

Connecting your first sources takes minutes. A useful, organized knowledge base typically takes shape within the first days — not a quarter-long migration project.
